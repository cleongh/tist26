 ALL OUR ATTEMPTS AT SUBTERFUGE HAD BEEN IN VAIN. With ice in my heart, I watched him prepare to defend me. His
intense concentration betrayed no hint of doubt, though he was outnumbered. I knew that we could expect no help—at this
moment, his family was fighting for their lives just as surely as he was for ours. Would I ever learn the outcome of
that other fight? Find out who the winners and the losers were? Would I live long enough for that? The odds of that
didn’t look so great. Black eyes, wild with their fierce craving for my death, watched for the moment when my
protector’s attention would be diverted. The moment when I would surely die. Somewhere, far, far away in the cold
forest, a wolf howled.
